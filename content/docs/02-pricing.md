# Pricing bands

All prices are guide bands. A final figure comes from the written treatment plan after an
examination, and the plan is sent by email before any treatment is booked. Nobody is asked
to commit to a cost at the front desk.

## Extras

X-rays are £25 each and are included in the new patient examination fee where clinically
indicated. A panoramic X-ray, taken at Docklands only, is £85.

There is no charge for a treatment plan, no charge for the two-week anti-wrinkle review,
and no charge for a familiarisation visit.

## What moves a quote within its band

Where the tooth is, how much of it is left, whether the work is being redone, and how long
the appointment needs to be. A crown on a heavily broken-down molar that needs a build-up
first sits at the top of the band. A straightforward crown on a well-prepared premolar sits
at the bottom.

The clinician gives you the specific figure at the examination, in writing, before anything
is booked.
