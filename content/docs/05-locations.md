# Locations, travel and parking

## Docklands

Meridian Docklands, Unit 4, Marsh Wall, London E14 9SH. This is the group's main site and
the largest, with six surgeries.

Getting there: five minutes' walk from South Quay on the DLR, and twelve minutes from
Canary Wharf on the Jubilee line. The 277 and D3 bus routes stop on Marsh Wall.

Parking: six patient bays behind the building, accessed from the service road off Marsh
Wall. They are first come, first served and free for two hours with a code from reception,
which you enter on the keypad by the barrier. The surrounding streets are pay and display
Monday to Saturday, 8am to 6.30pm. Docklands is outside the Congestion Charge zone but
inside the ULEZ.

Access: step-free throughout, automatic front door, and an accessible WC on the ground
floor. There is a hearing loop at the front desk.

## Shoreditch

Meridian Shoreditch, 118 Curtain Road, London EC2A 3AH. Three surgeries.

Getting there: four minutes from Old Street on the Northern line and eight minutes from
Shoreditch High Street on the Overground.

Parking: no patient parking at this site. The nearest car park is the NCP multi-storey on
Great Eastern Street, roughly a four-minute walk. Shoreditch sits inside both the
Congestion Charge zone and the ULEZ, so driving here on a weekday is expensive and we
generally suggest the tube.

Access: the surgeries are on the first floor and there is a lift. The lift is large enough
for a wheelchair but not for a stretcher.

## Clapham

Meridian Clapham, 7 Voltaire Road, London SW4 6DH. Three surgeries.

Getting there: six minutes from Clapham High Street on the Overground and nine minutes from
Clapham Common on the Northern line. The 88 and 345 stop on Clapham Road.

Access: step-free at the front and all surgeries are on the ground floor.

## Opening hours

Docklands: Monday to Friday 8am to 6pm, Saturday 9am to 2pm.
Shoreditch: Monday to Friday 8.30am to 6pm, Saturday 9am to 2pm.
Clapham: Monday to Thursday 9am to 6pm, Friday 9am to 5pm, Saturday 9am to 2pm.

All three sites are closed on Sundays and on bank holidays. The phones are answered from
8am on weekdays across all sites by the Docklands front desk.

## Moving between sites

Your records are shared across all three sites, so you can be seen at whichever is
convenient without transferring anything. Your usual clinician does not move with you,
though, so if you want to stay with the same person you need to book at their site.

## What is at which site

Docklands has general dentistry, hygiene, the full aesthetics list, and the group's only
panoramic X-ray machine. Shoreditch has general dentistry and hygiene. Clapham has general
dentistry and aesthetics, with hygiene run through a general surgery.
