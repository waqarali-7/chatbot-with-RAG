# New patients

## Registering

There is no registration fee and no waiting list to join. Book a new patient examination and
the registration is completed at that appointment. You do not need a referral and you do not
need to have been seen anywhere recently.

## The first appointment

Forty minutes. It covers a medical history, a full charting of the teeth and any existing
work, a periodontal screening, a soft tissue and jaw joint check, X-rays where clinically
indicated, and a conversation about what you actually want from your teeth.

You leave with a written treatment plan by email, usually the same day and always within
two working days. The plan lists each item with its band, and the order things should be
done in, so you can decide what to do and when.

Nothing is treated at the first appointment except an emergency. If something needs doing
urgently the clinician will say so and get you comfortable, and the rest is booked
separately.

## Transferring records

Ask your previous practice to email your records and recent X-rays to the referrals address
on the website. Most send them within a fortnight. You do not need to wait for them to be
booked in, and we will not delay your appointment for them.

If your previous practice charges for records, that is between you and them. Under UK data
protection rules they cannot charge for a first copy in most circumstances.

## Nervous patients

Tell the front desk when you book and the appointment goes with a clinician who runs longer
slots, usually Dr Okafor at Docklands.

You can come in first just to see the room and meet the dentist with nothing done at all.
That is a familiarisation visit, it takes fifteen minutes, and there is no charge. People
use it more than you would think and nobody finds it odd.

Say what specifically you find difficult, because the answers are different. Needles, the
drill sound, gagging, and not being able to stop are four different problems with four
different approaches.

## Children

Under-16s must be accompanied by an adult with parental responsibility for the whole
appointment. A child examination is £45.

First visits for small children are deliberately short and often amount to a ride in the
chair and a count of the teeth. That is the intention, not a wasted appointment.

## What we need from you when you book

A name, a contact number, an email address for the confirmation and the plan, and a rough
sense of what the appointment is for. That is all. We do not ask for card details to hold a
routine appointment.

## Referrals from other practices

We accept referrals by email to the referrals address on the website. A referred patient is
contacted within three working days. Endodontic referrals go to Dr Rana at Docklands.
