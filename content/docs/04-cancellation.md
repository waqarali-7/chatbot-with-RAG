# Cancellation, changes and missed appointments

## Notice

We ask for 24 hours' notice to change or cancel an appointment. For appointments longer
than 45 minutes, which includes in-surgery whitening, deep cleaning and most aesthetics
treatment, we ask for 48 hours.

Notice can be given by phone, by replying to the reminder text, or by email. The reply to
the reminder text is the fastest and reaches the right site automatically.

## Charges

A first missed appointment carries no charge. A second missed appointment within twelve
months is charged at 50% of the appointment fee. A third means we ask for a deposit before
booking anything longer than 30 minutes.

Short-notice cancellation, meaning inside the notice period, is treated the same as a missed
appointment for counting purposes.

The charge is waived for illness, bereavement, a caring emergency, or anything else that
would obviously count. Tell the front desk what happened and it comes off. Nobody is asked
for evidence.

## Deposits

A deposit is taken for in-surgery whitening, deep cleaning, and any aesthetics appointment
booked at Docklands on a Saturday, because Saturday aesthetics slots are the ones that go
unfilled when someone does not turn up.

The deposit is £75 and comes off the final bill. It is refunded in full if the appointment
is changed with the required notice, and is kept if it is not.

## Running late

If you are more than ten minutes late for a 20-minute appointment we will usually need to
rebook, because the surgery runs to the next patient. For a 40-minute appointment there is
more room and it is often still workable at fifteen minutes late.

Call ahead. The front desk will tell you honestly whether it is worth continuing your
journey, and would rather do that than have you arrive to be turned away.

## If we cancel

If a clinician is off sick we call as early as we can and offer the next available slot with
them, or an earlier slot with a colleague. If the cancellation was ours and you had taken
time off work, say so, and the rebooked appointment is prioritised.

## Waiting list and short-notice list

If nothing suitable is open we can add you to the short-notice list. When someone cancels we
text the list in order and the first person to reply takes the slot. Most people on the list
get an offer within eight working days, and it is free to be on it.

Say which sites and which days work for you when you join the list, because a list entry
that only works one afternoon a week rarely gets matched.
