# Common questions

## How soon can I be seen?

Routine examinations are usually available within a week at Docklands and Clapham, and
within two weeks at Shoreditch. New patient examinations run about two weeks across all
three sites. Emergency slots are released at 8am each weekday, two per site.

## Do I need an examination before hygiene?

Yes if you are new to us, or if your last examination with us was more than twelve months
ago. Existing patients seen within twelve months can book hygiene directly.

This is not an upsell. A hygienist cannot safely clean around a tooth nobody has looked at,
and undiagnosed decay under the gum line is exactly what a clean will not fix.

## Can I see a specific clinician?

Yes. Say who when you book. It can add a week or so to the wait depending on their list, and
more for a new patient appointment with Dr Rana.

## Do you do house calls or online consultations?

No home visits at all. Aesthetics consultations can be done by video first, but any
treatment requires an in-person assessment on a separate day. Dental problems cannot be
assessed by video and we will not try.

## What if I am pregnant?

Tell us when you book. Routine examinations and hygiene are safe throughout pregnancy and
the second trimester is the most comfortable time for anything longer.

Elective X-rays and whitening are deferred until after the birth. Facial aesthetics is not
carried out during pregnancy or while breastfeeding.

## Do you treat anxious patients?

Yes, and it is a large part of what Dr Okafor does. Ask for a longer appointment when you
book, and consider a familiarisation visit first, which is free.

## Can I bring someone with me?

Yes, one person. For aesthetics consultations we actively encourage it, because a second
opinion in the room tends to produce more realistic decisions.

## Do you have parking?

Docklands has six patient bays behind the building, free for two hours with a code from
reception. Shoreditch has none and the nearest is the Great Eastern Street multi-storey.

## Is there anywhere to leave a bike?

There are two Sheffield stands directly outside Docklands and a rack in the yard at
Shoreditch. Clapham has on-street stands about thirty metres along Voltaire Road.

## How do I get a copy of my records?

Email the practice manager. Records are released within one month at no charge, in line with
UK data protection rules. Ask for X-rays specifically if you want them, because they go as a
separate file.

## Do you have a complaints process?

Yes. Complaints go to the practice manager in writing, are acknowledged within three working
days, and are answered in full within twenty-one days. If you are not satisfied with the
outcome, the letter tells you how to take it to the Dental Complaints Service.

## Can I change my appointment by text?

Yes. Reply to the reminder text and it reaches the right site. That is the fastest route and
the one we would rather you used.

## Do you send reminders?

A confirmation email when you book and a text 48 hours before. You can opt out of either.

## Do you treat people who have not been to a dentist in years?

Routinely, and it is a much larger share of new patients than people assume. Nobody
comments on it, and the first appointment is an examination and a plan rather than
treatment, so there is no surprise on the day.
