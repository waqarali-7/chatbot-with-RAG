# Payment, insurance and plans

## How to pay

Card, Apple Pay and Google Pay at all three sites. Payment is taken at the end of the
appointment, not in advance, except where a deposit applies.

Treatment over £600 can be split, with half taken on the day of the first appointment and
the balance on completion. There is no charge for splitting a payment this way.

We do not take cash at Shoreditch. Docklands and Clapham take cash up to £500 per
transaction. We cannot take bank transfers for treatment, and we do not take cheques.

A receipt is emailed automatically after every payment. Itemised receipts with treatment
codes are what most insurers need, and those are produced as standard.

## Insurance

Meridian is not a registered provider with any insurer, so we do not bill insurers
directly and we cannot check your cover for you. You pay us and claim back.

The itemised receipt carries the treatment codes your policy will ask for. Patients on
Bupa, AXA, Denplan and WPE policies routinely claim back against Meridian receipts. If your
insurer wants a pre-treatment estimate, ask at the examination and the treatment plan is
formatted to suit.

## Membership plan

The Meridian Plan is £24.50 a month. It covers two examinations and two hygiene
appointments in a twelve-month period, all routine X-rays, and a 15% discount on
restorative treatment and 10% on whitening and aesthetics.

The plan runs on a rolling monthly direct debit with a three-month minimum term. After
that you can cancel any time with one month's notice. It does not cover treatment already
recommended before you joined, and it does not cover emergency assessments, which are
charged at the usual rate.

There is a family version at £22 a month per person for three or more people at the same
address.

## Finance

Interest-free finance over six or twelve months is available on treatment plans over
£1,000, subject to a credit check by our finance partner. Longer terms up to 36 months are
available with interest.

The application is completed on a tablet at the practice and takes about ten minutes for a
decision. A deposit of 10% is required on interest-free agreements.

## NHS

Meridian is a fully private group. We do not hold an NHS contract and cannot see patients
under NHS charges at any of the three sites. If you need NHS care, the NHS Find a Dentist
service is the right starting point, and we are happy to say so rather than waste your
time.

## Refunds

If a laboratory-made item fails within twelve months through no fault of the patient it is
remade at no charge. Refunds on completed treatment are considered case by case by the
practice manager and are not automatic.
