# Practice policies

## Consent

Nothing is treated without consent, and for anything beyond a routine filling that consent
is written. The treatment plan is the basis for it, so you have the costs and the options in
front of you before you sign anything.

Consent can be withdrawn at any point, including mid-appointment. Raise a hand and the
clinician stops. That is a normal thing to do and every clinician here works that way.

## Data and records

Clinical records are kept for eleven years after the last appointment, or until a patient's
twenty-fifth birthday for children, whichever is longer. That is the standard UK retention
period.

Records are stored in a UK-hosted clinical system. They are not shared outside the practice
except with your consent, or where a clinician is legally obliged to.

You can ask for a copy of your records at any time, at no charge, and it is released within
one month.

Marketing emails go out only to patients who opted in, and the unsubscribe link works
immediately and permanently.

## Chaperones

A chaperone is available for any appointment on request, with no explanation needed. Ask at
the front desk when you book or on the day.

## Complaints

Complaints go to the practice manager in writing, by email or letter. They are acknowledged
within three working days and answered in full within twenty-one days.

If the answer does not resolve it, the response letter sets out how to escalate, which for
private treatment is the Dental Complaints Service and for a registration concern is the
General Dental Council.

We would rather hear about a problem early. Most complaints are about waiting time,
communication about cost, or a bite that was not adjusted, and all three are quick to fix if
somebody says so.

## Infection control

Instruments are sterilised on site in a validated autoclave with daily testing and a
logged audit trail. Single-use items are used where they exist. The infection control policy
is available at reception on request.

## Zero tolerance

Abuse or threats towards staff end the appointment and the registration. This is not
negotiable and it applies at all three sites.

## Interpreters and accessibility

Between them the team speak English, Portuguese, Bengali, Polish and Cantonese, and the
front desk will arrange for a colleague who speaks your language to be present. A
professional interpreter can be arranged with a week's notice at no charge.

Docklands and Clapham are step-free. Shoreditch has a lift to the first floor. A hearing
loop is fitted at the Docklands front desk.

## Photography

Clinical photographs are taken for record keeping in most restorative and aesthetics cases,
and consent for that is separate and specific. Photographs are never used for marketing
without a further written consent that names that purpose.
