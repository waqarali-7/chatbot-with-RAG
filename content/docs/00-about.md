# About Meridian Dental & Aesthetics

Meridian Dental & Aesthetics is a private dental and facial aesthetics group with three
London locations: Docklands, Shoreditch and Clapham. The group has been operating since
2014 and sees around 400 patients a week across the three sites.

## What kind of practice this is

Fully private, general dentistry led, with a facial aesthetics service that grew out of
patient demand rather than being bolted on. There is no NHS contract at any site.

The practice is not a chain and is not corporate owned. Decisions about how long an
appointment runs are made by the clinician rather than by a booking target, which is why
appointment lengths here are longer than average and why the fees are what they are.

## Ownership and clinical governance

Meridian is a partnership between two principal dentists. Clinical governance is overseen by
a lead clinician at each site, and the group holds a monthly clinical governance meeting
covering audit, incidents and complaints.

All clinicians are registered with the General Dental Council. The aesthetics team are
registered nurses working under a prescriber, and the lead aesthetics practitioner is an
independent prescriber in her own right.

## Which site does what

Docklands is the largest site and the group's main location, with six surgeries. It offers
general dentistry, hygiene with a dedicated hygienist, the full facial aesthetics list, and
the group's only panoramic X-ray machine.

Shoreditch has three surgeries and offers general dentistry and hygiene. It does not offer
aesthetics.

Clapham has three surgeries and offers general dentistry and facial aesthetics. It does not
have a dedicated hygiene chair, so hygiene appointments at Clapham run through a general
surgery and take 40 minutes rather than 30.

## How the practice runs

Appointments are longer than the industry average by design. A routine examination is 20
minutes where 10 is common, and a new patient examination is 40.

Treatment is planned in writing before it is booked. Nobody is asked to agree to a cost in
the chair, and the treatment coordinators exist so that a long plan is discussed properly
rather than at the front desk with a queue behind you.

## Languages

Front desk and clinical staff between them speak English, Portuguese, Bengali, Polish and
Cantonese. If a patient needs a specific language, the front desk can arrange for a
colleague who speaks it to be present at the appointment, or book a professional interpreter
with a week's notice at no charge.
