# Services

## General dentistry

Routine examinations, fillings, extractions, crowns, bridges, veneers, inlays, onlays and
root canal treatment.

A routine examination takes 20 minutes. It covers a check of every tooth, a soft tissue
examination of the cheeks, tongue and floor of the mouth, a basic periodontal examination
score, and small bitewing X-rays where clinically indicated. Bitewings are usually taken
every two years for a low-risk patient and annually for someone with a history of decay.

A new patient examination takes 40 minutes. It adds a full charting of existing
restorations, a six-point periodontal chart if the basic screening flags anything, a jaw
joint check, and a written treatment plan emailed afterwards. The plan lists each item, the
band it falls in, and the order things should be done in.

Fillings are white composite as standard. Amalgam is no longer placed at any Meridian site.
A composite filling takes 30 to 45 minutes depending on how many surfaces are involved.

Root canal treatment on a front tooth is usually one appointment of 60 to 75 minutes. A
molar is usually two appointments of 90 minutes, two to three weeks apart, with a temporary
dressing in between. A molar root canal is followed by a crown in most cases, because the
remaining tooth structure is brittle.

Crowns and veneers are made by a laboratory in Kent and take two weeks. You leave the first
appointment with a temporary and come back for the fit, which takes 30 minutes.

## Hygiene

Scale and polish, air-flow stain removal, and periodontal maintenance.

A standard hygiene appointment is 30 minutes and covers ultrasonic scaling above and just
below the gum line, hand scaling where needed, and a polish. Air-flow adds 15 minutes and
uses a pressurised powder spray that lifts staining from tea, coffee, red wine and smoking
more effectively than a polish alone.

Deep cleaning, properly called root surface debridement, is carried out under local
anaesthetic and split across two appointments of 45 minutes, one per side of the mouth,
usually two weeks apart. It is followed by a review at eight to twelve weeks to re-chart
the pockets and see what has responded.

Periodontal maintenance for a patient with treated gum disease runs at three or four month
intervals rather than six, and each appointment is 40 minutes.

Hygiene is available at Docklands and Shoreditch with a dedicated hygienist. Clapham runs
hygiene through a general surgery, so those appointments are 40 minutes rather than 30.

## Facial aesthetics

Anti-wrinkle injections, dermal filler, skin boosters, chemical peels, and medical-grade
skincare consultations.

Every aesthetics patient has a consultation before any treatment. The consultation is 30
minutes, covers medical history, what you want to change, what is realistic, and the risks.
There is a mandatory two-week cooling-off period between a first consultation and a
first-time dermal filler treatment. Anti-wrinkle treatment can be carried out on the same
day as the consultation for a returning patient, but not for a first-time patient, who is
booked back after 48 hours.

Anti-wrinkle treatment takes 20 minutes and is reviewed at two weeks. The two-week review
is free and is when any adjustment is done.

Dermal filler appointments are 45 minutes for one area and 60 for two. Filler is
hyaluronic acid based and is reversible with hyaluronidase if there is a problem.

Skin boosters are a course of three, four weeks apart, each 30 minutes.

Chemical peels are 45 minutes. A course of four is usual for pigmentation, and peels are
not carried out on skin that has had recent sun exposure.

Aesthetics is available at Docklands and Clapham. Shoreditch does not offer aesthetics.

## Whitening

Take-home whitening with custom trays, and in-surgery whitening.

Take-home requires two appointments. The first, 30 minutes, takes impressions or a digital
scan. The second, 20 minutes, fits the trays and hands over the gel with instructions. The
gel is worn for two to four hours a day, or overnight for the lower-strength option, over
two to three weeks.

In-surgery whitening is a single appointment of 90 minutes and gets to a similar end point
in one visit, with more short-term sensitivity. Most patients who have in-surgery whitening
also take home trays for maintenance.

Whitening requires a dental examination within the previous six months. It cannot be
carried out on patients with active decay, untreated gum disease, or during pregnancy or
breastfeeding. It does not change the colour of crowns, veneers or white fillings, so
existing restorations at the front of the mouth may need replacing afterwards to match.

## Emergency appointments

Each site holds two emergency slots per weekday, released at 8am on the day. Emergency
appointments are 20 minutes and are for pain, swelling, trauma, or a lost filling or crown.

They are assessment appointments. The clinician will get you comfortable, which often means
dressing a tooth or draining a swelling, and book the definitive treatment separately.

Out of hours there is no on-call service. The answerphone gives the NHS 111 number, which
is the correct route for severe pain or facial swelling overnight or on a Sunday.
