# The clinical team

## Dr Aisha Rana, principal dentist, Docklands

Qualified at King's College London in 2009. GDC number withheld here but displayed at
reception and on the website.

Special interest in root canal treatment and complex restorative work, and takes the
group's referrals for molar endodontics. Also the clinical lead for the Docklands site.

Sees new patients on Mondays, Tuesdays and Thursdays. Her list runs about two weeks ahead
for a routine appointment and longer for a new patient examination.

## Dr Tom Okafor, dentist, Docklands

Qualified at Bristol in 2015. General dentistry with a focus on nervous patients. He runs
the practice's longer appointment slots for people who need more time, and does the
familiarisation visits.

Works Tuesday to Saturday. He is the clinician the front desk books anxious patients with
by default.

## Elena Marchetti, aesthetics practitioner, Docklands and Clapham

Registered nurse and independent prescriber with twelve years in facial aesthetics. Leads
the aesthetics service across the group and supervises the other aesthetics practitioner.

Consults at Docklands on Wednesdays and Fridays and at Clapham on Thursdays. Filler and
anti-wrinkle work is the bulk of her list.

## Dr Priya Nair, dentist, Shoreditch

Qualified at Manchester in 2013. General and cosmetic dentistry, and the group's lead for
whitening, so most take-home and in-surgery whitening cases are planned with her even when
another clinician carries them out.

Works Monday to Friday at Shoreditch.

## Jo Bennett, hygienist, Shoreditch

Fifteen years in periodontal care. Runs the Shoreditch hygiene list and the group's
periodontal maintenance programme, and takes the deep cleaning cases from all three sites.

Works Monday, Wednesday and Friday.

## Dr Sam Whitfield, dentist, Clapham

Qualified at Leeds in 2018. General dentistry and emergency care, and covers most of the
Clapham emergency slots.

Works Monday to Thursday and alternate Saturdays.

## Hana Suzuki, aesthetics practitioner, Clapham

Registered nurse with eight years in aesthetics, trained in Tokyo and London. Skin boosters
and chemical peels are her main list, and she works under Elena Marchetti's prescribing
supervision.

Works Monday, Tuesday and Saturday at Clapham.

## Requesting a specific clinician

Say who when you book. It usually adds a week or so to the wait, and more for Dr Rana's new
patient list. If it is urgent, the front desk will offer the first available clinician and
put you back with your usual one for the follow-up.

## Support team

There are eight dental nurses across the three sites, two treatment coordinators who talk
through longer plans and finance, and a practice manager who handles complaints and
records requests.
